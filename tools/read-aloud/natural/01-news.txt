# City Water Rates Will Rise Next Spring

The council voted on Tuesday to raise residential water rates by nine percent, the first increase since 2019. Officials said the money will pay for pipe repairs that have been delayed for years. The plan passed six to three after a long public hearing in 2024

## Key Takeaways
- Bills rise by about four dollars a month for a typical home
- Seniors on fixed incomes can apply for a discount
- The first repairs start in the oldest neighborhoods
- A new rate review is due in three years

## What Residents Said
More than forty people spoke at the hearing. Several asked why the city waited so long to fix the pipes, and others worried about renters who do not see the bill directly. One owner of a small laundry said her costs would climb faster than her prices.

## What Happens Next
The new rates take effect in April. The utility will mail a notice with every bill in February and post a simple calculator on its website so households can check their own numbers.
