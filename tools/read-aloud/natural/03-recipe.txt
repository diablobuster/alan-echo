# Weeknight Lentil Soup

This soup takes about forty minutes and feeds four people. It keeps well in the fridge for three days and tastes even better the next day.

## Ingredients
- One cup of dry red lentils
- One onion, chopped
- Two carrots, sliced thin
- Three cloves of garlic
- One can of crushed tomatoes
- Six cups of vegetable stock
- A spoon of ground cumin
- Salt and a squeeze of lemon

## Steps
1. Warm a little oil in a large pot over medium heat
2. Cook the onion and carrots until soft, about eight minutes
3. Add the garlic and cumin and stir for one minute
4. Pour in the lentils, tomatoes and stock
5. Simmer for twenty five minutes, stirring now and then
6. Season with salt and lemon before serving

## Tips
If the soup gets too thick, add a splash of water. For a smoother texture, blend half of it and stir it back into the pot. Crusty bread on the side makes it a full meal.
